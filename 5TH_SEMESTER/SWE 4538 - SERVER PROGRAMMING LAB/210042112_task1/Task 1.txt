Virtual Pet Playground is a playful API where users can adopt, care for and interact with virtual pets. Users can create a pet, monitor its stats, and perform actions like playing, or feeding the pet. Each action influences the pet's stats, creating a lively simulation where proper care keeps the pet happy but neglecting it might make it sick or run away!  
Requirements 
1. Create a pet: Users can create a new pet with a name, couple of images, age and species (like a bunny, puppy, or dragon). 
Initial stats: 
Hunger: 30 
Energy: 50 
Health: 100 
2. View pet stats: Retrieve the current stats of a pet using its ID. If the pet's energy is less than 0 or its hunger exceeds 50, the pet will either become sick or run away, respectively. 
3. Feed pet: Feeding the pet will decrease its hunger by 25 and increase its energy by 5. 
4. Play with pet: Playing with the pet will decrease its energy by 30 and increase its hunger by 25. 
5. Users can release their pet, removing it from the system. Additionally, if the pet's energy is below 0 and its hunger exceeds 50, the pet will be removed from the playground automatically
